Who We Are

Bridging Hardware and Intelligent Systems

At EZHUMIN TECHNOLOGIES, we operate at the intersection of physical hardware engineering and intelligent cloud-integrated software. Founded by expert firmware and systems engineers, we specialize in building highly resilient edge-computing infrastructure.

From safety-critical bare-metal code running on ultra-low-power microcontrollers to complex multi-threaded Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS) managing microgrids, we write firmware that is built to last.

Why Work With Us?

Hardware-Software Co-Design

We do not develop software in a vacuum. We write firmware with a deep understanding of analog signal conditioning, hardware constraints, and thermal limits.

Safety-Critical Standards

Our code structures adhere to strict guidelines (MISRA C compliance, defensive programming) to guarantee high uptime and protection against register lockups or memory leaks.

Power Optimization

We understand sleep states, clock prescalers, and interrupt-driven execution paths. We optimize your system down to micro-amperes to extend battery cycles.
